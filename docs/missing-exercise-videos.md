# Missing exact exercise videos

Reviewed: 2026-08-21

Four of 27 canonical exercises intentionally have no embedded video. Project 75 does not substitute a similar movement where the balance support or equipment would materially change what the user sees.

| Exercise | Why it remains missing | Rejected alternatives |
|---|---|---|
| Assisted Squat | The app specifies two hands on a solid fixed support. | Freedom Physical Therapy’s yoga-band-assisted squat and Dr. Carl Baird’s TRX squat both require different assistance equipment and mechanics. |
| Supported Goblet Squat | The app specifies a dumbbell in one hand position plus a stable support reachable by the free hand. | Standard unsupported goblet-squat tutorials omit the defining balance support. |
| Supported Reverse Lunge | The app specifies a stable support used only for balance. | Standard reverse-lunge demonstrations omit the support and can create a different balance demand. |
| Supported Split Squat | The app specifies a rear-foot-down stance and one-hand stable support. | Unsupported split squats and rear-foot-elevated Bulgarian split squats are different progressions. |

These exercises still include full written coaching and structured muscle metadata. A future video should only be added after its exact movement, source metadata, and privacy-enhanced embed are rechecked.
